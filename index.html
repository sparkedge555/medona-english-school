Our Story — Educating Bihar's Future Leaders

Medona English School stands as a premier institution in Triveniganj, Supaul, Bihar. Founded with an unwavering commitment to quality education, we have been shaping young minds and nurturing talent in the region.

Affiliated with the Y.L. Karan & Sons Educational Trust, registered under the Government of Bihar, our school follows a holistic curriculum that blends academic rigor with personality development, sports, and extracurricular activities.

Our UDISE Code 10061101317 reflects our recognition and compliance with national education standards. We offer classes from Nursery to 8th Standard in a safe, modern, and inspiring environment.

Y.L. Karan & Sons Educational Trust

Registered under the Government of Bihar, the Trust is committed to making quality education accessible to every child in the region. Our values of integrity, excellence, and community guide everything we do.

World-Class Amenities

Built for Learning, Designed for Excellence

Every facility at Medona English School is thoughtfully designed to support holistic student development.

Airy Classrooms

Our spacious, well-ventilated classrooms are designed to provide a comfortable and focused learning environment. Equipped with proper lighting, fans, and learning aids for all classes from Nursery to 8th.

e-Science Lab

Our modern e-Science Laboratory integrates digital technology with hands-on experiments. Students explore scientific concepts through interactive simulations, models, and real experiments under expert supervision.

Computer Lab

Fully equipped computer laboratory with modern systems and internet access. Students develop digital literacy, coding fundamentals, and essential tech skills crucial for the 21st century.

Library & Study Materials

Our well-stocked library houses hundreds of books, reference materials, magazines, and educational resources. Curated study materials are also provided to all students to supplement classroom learning.

CCTV Surveillance

Our campus is equipped with 24×7 CCTV surveillance cameras at all key points. This ensures the complete safety and security of every student on campus, giving parents absolute peace of mind.

Qualified Teachers

Our team of highly qualified, trained, and experienced educators are passionate about teaching. They use modern pedagogical methods to make learning engaging, effective, and enjoyable for every student.

24×7 Doubt Support

Students can access round-the-clock academic support through our dedicated doubt-clearing helpline and digital channels. No question is too small — we ensure every student fully understands their lessons.

Co-curricular Activities

We believe in holistic development. Regular co-curricular activities including sports, drawing, music, and cultural events help students explore their talents beyond academics.
